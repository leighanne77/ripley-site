Ripley Decision Advantage · Palo Alto, CA · New as of July 2026 · Founder & architect

A multi-agent decision-support system that prices statistically significant extreme-weather risk for the assets defense depends on — shipyards, ports, mineral processing, and the energy that powers the perimeter.

AI tools for patriotic capital

Palo Alto, CA · May 2026–present · AI Solution Architect · bigdin.net (team access only)

Defense Investor Network (DIN) and Defense Angel Network (DAN): a production investor-relations platform for a dual-use defense investor team — maritime industrial base, critical-mineral sovereignty, energy resilience. Architected and shipped solo, live with a real user base.
