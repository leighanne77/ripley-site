Current work

Extreme-weather risk for defense-adjacent assets

Ripley Decision Advantage · Palo Alto, CA · New as of July 2026 · Founder & architect

A multi-agent decision-support system that prices statistically significant extreme-weather risk for the assets defense depends on — shipyards, ports, mineral processing, and the energy that powers the perimeter.

  • 20+ data loaders on a 1–6 hour refresh, fusing NOAA, OpenFEMA and ERDDAP ground truth with an economic ledger and 45+ nature-based-solutions datasets.
  • Orchestrated on Google’s ADK over the Agent-to-Agent protocol, with custom validation and resilience layers.
  • Isolated and API-first. It never plugs into a financial core — it exports verifiable data a human carries into their own models.

AI tools for patriotic capital

Palo Alto, CA · May 2026–present · AI Solution Architect · www.defenseinvestornetwork.net (member access only)

Defense Investor Network (DIN) and Defense Angel Network (DAN): a production investor-relations platform for a dual-use defense investor team — maritime industrial base, critical-mineral sovereignty, energy resilience. Architected and shipped solo, live with a real user base.

  • A graph-backed warm-introduction engine on Neo4j scores relationship paths deterministically and auditably, behind hard safety gates.
  • PII governance mapped to NIST AI RMF and SR 11-7: three-tier privacy at the query layer, owner-scoped writes, Fernet encryption, hashed-payload audit trail.
  • Data layer right-sized deliberately — PostgreSQL and recursive CTEs first, Spark rejected as over-scaled for the volume.
