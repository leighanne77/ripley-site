Current work

Extreme-weather risk for defense-adjacent assets

Ripley Decision Advantage · Palo Alto, CA · New as of July 2026 · Founder & architect

Say you run a port. A Katrina-sized storm could shut it down for weeks. Before you build anything, my system shows what that would cost you and what would protect you: restoring the marsh offshore, adding backup power, or raising the substations above the flood line. It prices each option, shows how others have paid for it, and shows who else in town gains if it works. Then you decide, because you answer for it.

A multi-agent decision-support system that prices statistically significant extreme-weather risk for the assets defense depends on — shipyards, ports, mineral processing, and the energy that powers the perimeter.

  • 20+ data loaders on a 1–6 hour refresh, fusing NOAA, OpenFEMA and ERDDAP ground truth with an economic ledger and 45+ nature-based-solutions datasets.
  • Orchestrated on Google’s ADK over the Agent-to-Agent protocol, with custom validation and resilience layers.
  • Isolated and API-first. It never plugs into a financial core — it exports verifiable data a human carries into their own models.

AI tools for patriotic capital

Palo Alto, CA · May 2026–present · AI Solution Architect · www.defenseinvestornetwork.net (member access only)

Defense Investor Network (DIN) and Defense Angel Network (DAN): a production, voice-first investor-relations CRM for a dual-use defense investor team — maritime industrial base, critical-mineral sovereignty, energy resilience. Architected and shipped solo; live on web and iPhone with the team using it daily.

  • A warm-introduction engine scores relationship paths deterministically and auditably, behind hard safety gates. No model decides who can see what or which route is best.
  • PII governance mapped to NIST AI RMF and SR 11-7: three-tier privacy enforced at the query layer, owner-scoped writes, a hashed-payload audit trail, and Fernet encryption of stored credentials.
  • Data layer right-sized on purpose: PostgreSQL recursive queries for the graph, Neo4j deferred behind a stated scale trigger, Spark rejected as over-scaled.
  • Built with an AI coding agent under enforced governance: every commit cites its plan, the builder cannot approve its own work, and an independent owner-run checker re-verifies each change.
