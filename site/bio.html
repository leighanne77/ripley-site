Leigh Anne Miller

A decade-plus in engineering, the last four specializing in multi-agent systems — and before that, a classically trained international security and foreign policy analyst. The two halves are the practice: systems built to survive audit, for decisions that carry real consequence.

AI/ML engineering

02

International security and foreign policy

She started as a classically trained analyst — under Dr. Pfaltzgraff at Tufts, then with Dr. Strmecki at the Smith Richardson Foundation, whose board has included Rumsfeld, Brzezinski and Woolsey, and today McMaster and Keane.
