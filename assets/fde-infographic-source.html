In production · live LP data
The customer problem
  • Traditional CRMs fail at the moment of capture. Partners don't type notes after a meeting, so the data goes stale and the tool quietly dies.
  • "Who do we know who knows this LP?" was tribal knowledge. You couldn't query it.
  • Sensitive LP data: regulator-grade scrutiny from day one.
What I shipped
  • Voice capture on iPhone (TestFlight): speak it, get a structured, access-controlled record.
  • Warm-intro engine: deterministic path scoring, affinity × connection ÷ hops, ≤2 hops, blocklist + consent gates the model cannot override.
  • Policy engine on every tool call: deny-by-default, fail-closed; destructive ops need a signed, expiring confirm token.
  • PII core: visible / redacted / hidden tiers at the query layer; hashed audit with field-level diffs; mapped to NIST AI RMF · SR 11-7.
  • AVA thesis testing (pass · conditional · reject, citation-first) and ARA sealed sharing: answers plus cryptographic receipts, never records.

QHow do you manage model costs?

Route each call to the cheapest place that meets the bar, and meter everything.

CALLtask · data · latency
ON-PREM GPUOllama · 7–14B models on an RTX 4060 Ti · $0 marginal · data stays home
FAST CLOUDGroq · low-latency voice-to-text · pay per use
FRONTIER CLOUDClaude · hard reasoning, customer-facing answers · only where it earns it

On-prem runs coding assist, the local Llama judge and bulk jobs. DESS per-user token + voice budgets; web-search spend capped and audited. Right-size infra too: PostgreSQL, not Spark.
